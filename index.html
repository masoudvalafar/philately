Persia’s mail in the reign of Ahmad Shah, 1909–1925

تاریخ پستی ایران در دوران احمدشاه قاجار

Revolution, foreign occupation and famine all left their marks on the post. This site studies them through covers: where each letter went, what it cost, who stamped it and who read it on the way.

On this site

  • EducationWhat you will find on a typical cover of the era: addresses, manuscript dates, postmarks, postbox, postage due and registration marks.
  • RatesPostal rates for letters, registration, receipts and foreign mail, each shown on a cover that paid it.
  • CensorsWho read the mail, and when: 70 censored items from nine sources, from the first Persian controls of 1910 to Reza Khan’s army.
  • ForgeriesCommon and less common forgeries, and how to tell them apart.
  • CollectionNotable items: the many ways to make up a rate, charity stamps, mixed frankings and more.
  • ResourcesBooks, catalogues and societies for the postal history of Iran.

An ongoing study

I am Masoud Valafar, and I collect the postal history of Iran from 1909 to 1925, the reign of Ahmad Shah. The period was one of constant upheaval, and the post shows it: stamps ran out and were revalued by hand, routes bent around war zones, and letters were opened by one authority after another.

This site is a work in progress. If you can correct a reading, date a mark or add an example, please write to me at mvalafar84@gmail.com.
