Coat of Arms Issue - 1909

Item 40198

Stamps: P421, P423, P424, P429

4/3/1911 a cover from Tehran to Isfahan franked with 1ch, 3ch, 6ch, and 1kr Coat of Arms issue of 1909. The cover also bears the Tehran control mark CP-03. Sometimes, receivers would add notes on the cover and the "nomre" manuscript in farsi seems to be added by the receiver.

See this cover in Censors

Item 40140

A registered cover sent from Koum on 1/6/1910 to Tehran, received on 4/6/1910. The cover is franked with two 10ch and one a 2ch coat of arms issue of 1909 for a total of 22ch, 16ch registration and 6ch postal rate.

The First Portrait of Ahmad Shah Issue - 1911

Item 40022

Stamps: P454, P469

A cover from Dalaki to Schiraz, received on 15/12/17. The cover is franked with 1ch and 5ch Ahmad Shah portrait issue of 1911.

Item 40144

Stamps: P454, P455, P470

A cover sent from Yezd on 7/4/21 to Kerman, received on 17/5/21. The manuscript and the postmark on the cover show that it was mistakenly rounted to Bandar Abbas and was forwarded to Kerman from there. The cover is tied with a 1ch, a 2ch and eight 6ch Ahmad Shah portrait issue of 1911 for a total of 30ch (This cover weighed 17 Miskal)

Item 40162

Stamps: P454, P455, P474

This cover was sent from Tauris on 16/1/22 to Tehran. The cover arrived in Tehran on 12/2/22 but was returned to Tauris due to the reciever moving to Europe. As such, the cover was returned to Tauris on 1/3/22. However, on 8/3/22 it was returned to Tehran yet again as the sender had moved to Tehran from Tauris. It was received in Tehran on 25/3/22 and was delivered to the original sender. The cover is franked with a 1ch, a 2ch, and a 3ch Ahmad Shah portrait issue of 1911 for 6ch.
