To Hamadan

A printed New Year (Nowruz 1302) greeting of March 1923, wishing Iran freedom and independence, addressed to Mirza Massih Khan at Hamadan. HAMADAN (ARRIVÉE) postmark of 27 March 1923. The CP-65 strike across the imprinted stamp cannot be a censorship of 1923, because the mark is dated 1303 (1924): like the card Mossavar-Rahmani illustrates, it was added later by Massih Khan, who kept the handstamp.
