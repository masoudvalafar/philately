Newspaper wrapper

Addressed to Mirza Massih Khan, with a HAMADAN postmark of 23 April (year not legible). The CP-65 strike across the 1ch stamp is one of the many Massih Khan applied to mail addressed to him; if the year is 1924, it falls in the one month of recorded genuine use.
